## Executive Summary & Landscape Assessment

1. **Direct 100% Free Hardware to Unaffiliated Individual Adults in India is Virtually Non-Existent through Traditional Welfare:** Central and state schemes (such as UP DigiShakti) exclusively target **currently enrolled students** via institutional ERP uploads, and provide tablets/smartphones rather than laptops.
2. **Beware of Virulent Phishing Scams:** All online claims of a central "PM Free Laptop Scheme" or "National Student Laptop Scheme" are fraudulent and have been repeatedly debunked by the **Press Information Bureau (PIB)**.
3. **The Most Viable Path to High-End Hardware is Open-Source Engineering Fellowships & Equipment Stipends:** Programs like the **Linux Foundation (LFX) Mentorship**, **Google Summer of Code (GSoC)**, and **Outreachy** do **not** require university enrollment, actively welcome Indian developers and freelancers, and disburse between **$1,300 USD and $7,000 USD** (₹1.08L to ₹5.8L INR) in unrestricted stipends that can immediately purchase a developer-grade laptop.
4. **NGO & CSR Avenues Require Structured Outreach:** Leading Indian tech NGOs (e.g., NASSCOM Foundation, Digital Empowerment Foundation) primarily allocate surplus corporate laptops to community labs or schools. Securing a refurbished device as an individual requires a formal direct pitch offering skills (e.g., conducting digital literacy or AI training) in exchange for community hardware.

## Top 10 Highest-Probability India-Relevant Leads

Ranked by likelihood of securing a working device or equivalent funding:

1. **Linux Foundation Mentorship (LFX Mentorship)**  
   * **Why Ranked #1:** Official, recurring, 100% legitimate open-source fellowship. Open to Indian residents and non-students/graduates. Delivers an unrestricted cash stipend adjusted for India ($1,300 USD / ~₹1,08,000 INR), which covers a mid-to-high-spec development laptop.  
   * **Key Limitation:** Competitive technical selection; requires contributing patches/code samples to open-source repositories during the application window.
2. **Google Summer of Code (GSoC) — Contributor Track**  
   * **Why Ranked #2:** Since 2022, GSoC is permanently open to **all adult developers (18+)**, removing the college enrollment requirement. The Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) stipend for India ranges from ₹71,250 (small project) to ₹2,85,000 (large project).  
   * **Key Limitation:** Annual cohort cycle (applications open early in the calendar year); requires writing a detailed technical project proposal.
3. **Outreachy Internship**  
   * **Why Ranked #3:** Designed for individuals facing systemic barriers in tech; explicitly welcomes non-students and part-time freelancers. Offers a massive $7,000 USD (~₹5,80,000 INR) stipend.  
   * **Key Limitation:** Requires committing 30 hours/week during the internship term (must not hold a concurrent full-time job); highly competitive initial essay and contribution phase.
4. **Digital Empowerment Foundation (DEF) — Digital Daan / CIRC Initiative**  
   * **Why Ranked #4:** Based in New Delhi with extensive grassroots operations across North India (including UP). Collects and refurbishes laptops/desktops for digital access. An MBA/EE graduate can apply to become a "SoochnaPreneur" or digital trainer, which includes hardware provisioning.  
   * **Key Limitation:** Not an automated individual giveaway; requires personal engagement, mutual value, or community leadership commitment.
5. **Major League Hacking (MLH) Fellowship — Open Source Track**  
   * **Why Ranked #5:** Fully remote 12-week fellowship open to Indian early-career developers. Provides needs-based educational stipends.  
   * **Key Limitation:** Variable stipend amounts based on corporate track sponsorship; demands rigorous coding review and technical interview.
6. **CIGMA Laptop Bank (CIGMA Foundation Trust)**  
   * **Why Ranked #6:** One of the few verified Indian non-profit initiatives specifically running a refurbished laptop lending library for job readiness and graduates.  
   * **Key Limitation:** Operates as a **laptop loan / deposit model** rather than an outright gift; recipient must provide a caution deposit or original documents; historically prioritizes Karnataka/Bangalore residents and Zakat-eligible candidates.
7. **Gitcoin Grants / Public Goods Matching Rounds**  
   * **Why Ranked #7:** If building open-source AI tooling, libraries, or community public goods, developers worldwide (including India) can list their project on Gitcoin Grants Stack to receive quadratic funding matching pool grants.  
   * **Key Limitation:** Requires an active Web3/open-source codebase and Gitcoin Passport Sybil-verification score.
8. **NASSCOM Foundation (BiG Bridge / CSR Hardware Partnerships)**  
   * **Why Ranked #8:** The premier IT-industry CSR body facilitating corporate surplus laptop refurbishment and distribution across India.  
   * **Key Limitation:** Officially directs hardware to partner NGOs, rural schools, and community centers; direct individual requests are evaluated strictly on exceptional ad-hoc merit or volunteer trainer alignment.
9. **American Library (American Center) & Delhi Public Library Tech Access**  
   * **Why Ranked #9:** Verified on-site computer and high-speed Wi-Fi access in Central Delhi for ₹400/year membership. Serves as an immediate zero-hardware fallback for remote job applications, freelance deliverables, and research.  
   * **Key Limitation:** In-person usage only; devices cannot be checked out to take home.
10. **Crowdfunding on Milaap / Ketto (Targeted Freelancer/Upskilling Campaign)**  
    * **Why Ranked #10:** Verified Indian crowdfunding platforms regularly host student and early-career developer equipment drives that raise ₹30,000–₹50,000 INR from community networks.  
    * **Key Limitation:** Requires personal promotional effort across social networks; platform takes a processing fee.

## Legitimate Foreign Programmes NOT Available to Indian Residents

Many viral articles recommend foreign non-profit hardware programs. The following organizations are verified non-profits, but **strictly exclude** residents of India:

1. **PCs for People** ([pcsforpeople.org](https://www.pcsforpeople.org))  
   * **Why Ineligible:** Restricted strictly to United States domestic addresses. Requires verifiable participation in US federal/state poverty assistance programs (SNAP, Medicaid, SSI) or proof of income below 200% of US Federal Poverty Guidelines.
2. **Computers with Causes** ([computerswithcauses.net](https://www.computerswithcauses.net))  
   * **Why Ineligible:** Operates exclusively within the 50 US states. Does not export hardware internationally due to logistics and warranty constraints.
3. **Human-I-T** ([human-i-t.org](https://www.human-i-t.org))  
   * **Why Ineligible:** Domestic US shipping only. Verification mandates US zip codes and federal income qualification documentation.
4. **The Turing Trust** ([turingtrust.co.uk](https://turingtrust.co.uk))  
   * **Why Ineligible:** Focuses exclusively on institutional bulk shipments to schools and educational organizations in Sub-Saharan Africa (predominantly Malawi). They do not supply laptops to individuals or to India.
5. **Computer Aid International** ([computeraid.org](https://www.computeraid.org))  
   * **Why Ineligible:** Explicitly maintains an organization-only policy (*"We do not provide computers directly to individuals"*). Supplies containerized batches to vetted schools, universities, and registered NGOs.
6. **World Computer Exchange (WCE)** ([worldcomputerexchange.org](https://www.worldcomputerexchange.org))  
   * **Why Ineligible:** Explicit stated policy: *"We do not provide computers to individuals."* Hardware is shipped only in bulk to registered non-profits, school districts, and community hubs.
7. **Legal & Import Barrier (DGFT India Regulations):**  
   * Even if a foreign donor offers to ship an individual used laptop from abroad, India’s **Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)** classifies second-hand/refurbished electronics as **restricted goods**. Individual couriers without Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) clearance and commercial import licenses face immediate seizure or customs duty/IGST tariffs exceeding 40–77% of the original product value at the foreign post office.

## Government Fact Check: Debunked Rumours vs. State Schemes

### 1. Debunked Scams: "PM Free Laptop Scheme" / "National Student Laptop Scheme"
* **The Viral Rumour:** Messages circulating across WhatsApp, Telegram, and spam blogs claim that the Government of India or the Ministry of Education is distributing 500,000+ free laptops to all youth/graduates under a nationwide "PM Laptop Yojana" via unofficial links (often hosted on `.blogspot.com`, `.xyz`, or unverified `.in` domains).
* **Official Reality:** **PIB Fact Check has repeatedly verified that NO such scheme exists.** The Central Government has not sanctioned any nationwide free laptop distribution.
* **Citation:** Official PIB Fact Check Advisories on X ([@PIBFactCheck](https://twitter.com/PIBFactCheck)) and [pib.gov.in](https://pib.gov.in).
* **Scam Mechanics:** These sites harvest Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, and bank details, or charge an upfront "courier fee" (₹499–₹999) via UPI. **Never transfer money or share OTPs for any government scheme.**

### 2. The Legitimate UP State Programme: DigiShakti / Swami Vivekanand Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana
* **Official Portal:** [digishakti.up.gov.in](https://digishakti.up.gov.in/)
* **Target Beneficiaries:** Regular students **currently enrolled** in recognized UP universities, colleges, ITIs, polytechnics, or state skill development courses.
* **Devices Distributed:** **Tablets and Smartphones only** (Samsung, Acer, Lava), **NOT** general laptops.
* **Application Mechanism:** There is **NO individual online registration form** on the portal. College administrators directly upload enrolled student rosters. The applicant (26-year-old postgraduate, completed B.Tech/MBA) is **ineligible** unless actively enrolled in a government-recognized vocational training course.

### 3. Other State Government Laptop Schemes (Not Applicable to UP Residents)
* **Madhya Pradesh Laptop Supply Scheme (`myscheme.gov.in`):** Requires 80%+ in MP State 12th Board.
* **Goa / Odisha / Andhra Pradesh Schemes:** Restricted strictly to SC/ST domiciles or persons with benchmark disabilities (>40% visual/locomotor impairment).

- [ ] **CIGMA Foundation:** Send Outreach Email 3 to `info@cigmafoundation.org` to verify geographic and guarantor constraints.
- [ ] **Alma Mater Alumni Cell (B.Tech / MBA Institutes):** Contact your university Dean of Student Welfare / Alumni Association. Many engineering colleges maintain an alumni emergency fund or surplus labs that lend workstations to unplaced/graduated alumni.

### Monitor
- [ ] **Major League Hacking (MLH) Fellowship:** Monitor [fellowship.mlh.io](https://fellowship.mlh.io/) for cohort batch releases.
- [ ] **Gitcoin Grants:** Monitor [gitcoin.co](https://gitcoin.co/) quarterly matching cycles for Open Source Software (OSS) rounds.
- [ ] **UP Skill Development Mission (UPSDM):** If open to enrolling in a short-term certified advanced skill program under UPSDM in UP, verify whether that specific training batch receives tablet distribution under DigiShakti.

### Discard / Avoid
- [ ] **Discard all "PM Free Laptop Scheme 2026" / "National Student Laptop Scheme" links:** These are fraudulent phishing scams.
- [ ] **Discard WhatsApp/Telegram Google Forms:** Any portal demanding upfront payments, courier charges, or Aadhaar OTPs for a "free laptop" is fraudulent.
- [ ] **Discard US-based giveaway sites (PCs for People, Human-I-T):** They do not ship internationally to India and used electronics cannot pass Indian customs without commercial DGFT clearance.
